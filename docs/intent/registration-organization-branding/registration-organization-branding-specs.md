# Registration Organization Branding Requirements

- [ ] **REG-BRAND-001**: When an applicant selects one of APSG, APSG Youth Wing, CWDI, or CWDI Youth Wing, the registration details and review views shall display exactly one parent-organization logo and name alongside the exact selected membership label.
- [ ] **REG-BRAND-003**: When a registration is successfully completed, the confirmation view shall derive its branding from the completed submission result and display only that organization's logo and name with the submitted membership label, including its Youth Wing designation when selected, regardless of a subsequent form reset or selection state.
- [ ] **REG-BRAND-004**: When an applicant changes the selected membership before submission, the details and review views shall reflect the current single selection and shall not retain the prior organization's logo or membership label.
- [ ] **REG-BRAND-005**: When an organization logo is rendered on the registration page, it shall have organization-specific alternative text, its organization name shall remain visible as text, and the logo and membership label shall remain legible without horizontal overflow at narrow viewport widths.
- [ ] **REG-BRAND-006**: If a local organization logo asset fails to load on the registration page, the organization name shall remain visible and the failed image shall not be replaced with another organization's logo.
- [ ] **REG-BRAND-007**: If no membership is selected, the registration details and review views shall not display an organization logo or membership label.
