# Public Registration Field Requirements

- [ ] **REG-FORM-001**: On the public APSG/CWDI registration page, the PVC/VIN and APC card number labels shall omit required markers and “Optional” suffixes, and the fields shall not block review or submission when left blank.
- [ ] **REG-FORM-002**: When an applicant provides either optional number, the registration page shall include that value in the selected organization's registration record; when omitted, it shall submit an empty string and display “Not provided” in review.
- [ ] **REG-FORM-003**: When an existing member record is reused and either optional number is omitted, the registration flow shall preserve the value already stored on that record.
- [ ] **REG-FORM-004**: On the public APSG/CWDI registration page, PVC and APC document uploads shall remain optional and shall not make their corresponding number fields required.
- [ ] **REG-FORM-005**: On the details step for APSG, APSG Youth Wing, CWDI, and CWDI Youth Wing registration, Personal Information shall include an optional Middle Name text input with additional-name autocomplete and an optional Gender select with Male, Female, and Prefer not to say choices and an empty initial value.
- [ ] **REG-FORM-006**: When registration review is opened, the page shall show the entered Middle Name and Gender as text or “Not provided” when blank; a successful new-registration receipt shall include a provided Middle Name between first and last name.
- [ ] **REG-FORM-007**: When an applicant selects a PVC or APC document, the public registration page shall accept only JPG, PNG or PDF files of at most 5 MB and show a field-specific accessible error that prevents review and submission until corrected.
- [ ] **REG-FORM-008**: On desktop and mobile registration details, each document input shall retain a visible native Choose file button with green background, white text, keyboard focus indication and a target of at least 44 px; its filename and format/size status shall fit the available width and update on selection and form reset.
- [ ] **REG-FORM-009**: When a new APSG or CWDI member is registering, the details step shall require a valid Country of Residence and shall initially select Nigeria; the applicant shall be able to change the selection, and the page shall not infer or change it from the phone calling code, browser location, locale, IP address, or legacy member data.
- [ ] **REG-FORM-010**: On the registration details step, Country of Residence and the phone calling code shall be independent controls; the phone calling-code selector shall be keyboard and screen-reader accessible, shall display each country's calling code before its name in the option label, shall default to Nigeria (+234), and shall not change the selected residence country.
- [ ] **REG-FORM-011**: When Country of Residence is Nigeria for a new member, the form shall retain the existing required Nigerian State, LGA/LCDA, and Ward controls, cascading options, and Nigerian registration-number flow.
- [ ] **REG-FORM-012**: When Country of Residence is outside Nigeria for a new member, the form shall require current City and State/Province fields and shall present Nigerian State, LGA/LCDA, and Ward only as a separate optional Nigerian home-location group.
- [ ] **REG-FORM-013**: Before review, lookup, or submission, registration and shared member phone flows shall strictly parse the phone using the locally bundled full international numbering metadata, validate its country-specific digit pattern, normalize valid input to E.164, and use that canonical value; malformed, invalid, or calling-code-mismatched numbers shall block progression and produce an accessible field-specific error.
- [ ] **REG-FORM-014**: When registration review is opened, it shall show the selected country and E.164 phone; for a non-Nigeria residence it shall show current country, city, and state/province separately from any provided Nigerian home State, LGA/LCDA, and Ward.
- [ ] **REG-FORM-015**: When a new member registration is submitted, the form payload shall include the selected residence country code, applicable current city and state/province, Nigerian location values when provided, and the E.164 phone; it shall not include a browser-selected Diaspora chapter or registration number.
- [ ] **REG-FORM-016**: When local metadata is used to populate residence-country and calling-code choices, the registration page shall load that metadata from the project’s local assets and shall not require browser geolocation or a runtime external country-directory request.
- [ ] **REG-FORM-017**: When Country of Residence is outside Nigeria, the APSG/CWDI details form shall show one optional Nigerian contact input fixed to `+234`, accept a valid Nigerian national or `+234` number, normalize it to canonical E.164 before review or submission, and shall not show that input to a Nigeria resident.
- [ ] **REG-FORM-018**: When a diaspora applicant has entered a valid Nigerian contact number distinct from the primary phone, the form shall show an accessible preferred-contact choice between the primary and Nigerian numbers and require a selection before review or submission; a missing selection shall show a visible correction message associated with the choice group, the choice shall clear when the secondary number becomes blank or invalid, and a subsequently valid number shall require a new choice; the choice shall be conditionally validated and shall not be unconditionally marked required in HTML.
- [ ] **REG-FORM-019**: If the Nigerian contact input is malformed, partial, uses a non-Nigerian calling code, or canonicalizes to the primary phone, the form shall block review and submission, retain the entered value for correction, clear any existing preference, and show an accessible field-specific error without starting a lookup from the Nigerian number.
- [ ] **REG-FORM-020**: When the applicant changes residence country, the form shall clear current city and state/province; when the change crosses the Nigeria boundary it shall also clear State/LGA/Ward, while a change between two non-Nigeria countries shall retain Nigerian home-location values.
- [ ] **REG-FORM-021**: When residence changes to Nigeria, the form shall hide and exclude the Nigerian contact number and preference from review and submission while retaining them only in unsent form state; when residence is outside Nigeria, the optional pair shall be submitted only for a new member when the secondary number and preference are valid, and both values shall be omitted when blank.
- [ ] **REG-FORM-022**: When registration review is opened, it shall show the canonical primary phone and, only when supplied and valid, the canonical Nigerian contact number and the selected preferred-contact label; it shall omit an absent Nigerian number and preference rather than displaying a placeholder for them.
- [ ] **REG-FORM-023**: When a new member proceeds to review or submission, the form shall require a valid primary phone independently of the optional Nigerian contact number and shall not allow the secondary number to satisfy or replace the primary-phone requirement.
- [ ] **REG-FORM-024**: When a diaspora applicant has supplied at least one Nigerian home-location value, review shall show a separately labeled Nigerian home-location section and display “Not provided” for each missing optional State/LGA/Ward value; when all are blank, review shall omit the section.
