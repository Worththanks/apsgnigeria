# Member Portal Requirements

## Login and recovery

- [ ] **PORTAL-LOGIN-001**: When a member submits an APSG or CWDI registration number and a valid canonical E.164 phone number, the member sign-in flow shall preserve the existing sign-in interaction and verify both values through an organization-aware session endpoint that queries only the registry selected by the registration-number prefix; APSG shall use `apsg_member_login_by_reg_phone`, CWDI shall use `cwdi_member_login_by_reg_phone`, and the browser shall not read either member table to verify a login.
- [ ] **PORTAL-LOGIN-002**: After successful APSG or CWDI member verification outside localhost preview, the session shall retain the matched registry source and a signed member credential valid for 30 minutes, and the member-details page shall refresh the record from that same registry; an expired credential shall require the member to sign in again before a write.
- [ ] **PORTAL-LOGIN-003**: When the member session endpoint issues a credential, the credential shall bind issuer, audience, member ID, organization, permitted basic-profile/photo-write scope, issue time and expiry, and shall exclude the member's raw registration number and phone number.
- [ ] **PORTAL-LOGIN-004**: If member sign-in verification fails or is rate-limited, then the sign-in flow shall show a generic failure without revealing whether a registration number or phone number exists and shall not issue a write credential.
- [ ] **PORTAL-LOGIN-005**: When the registration candidate flow has verified an existing member for general membership and opens the production member portal, the portal session endpoint shall receive the candidate's canonical E.164 phone, re-verify the registration number and phone using the selected registry's full-number rules, issue the scoped member credential, and complete the handoff without another login prompt; if credential issuance fails, the page shall keep the verified candidate and show a retryable portal-entry error; localhost preview shall retain its verified view-only handoff without a write-capable credential.
- [ ] **PORTAL-LOGIN-006**: In member login and recovery phone confirmation, the page shall provide an accessible calling-code selector backed by the same local international phone metadata as registration, display each calling code before its country name, default it to Nigeria (`NG`, `+234`), accept a national phone with its selected calling code or a complete `+` E.164 number, and validate to one canonical E.164 value before a request; the calling-code selector shall remain independent from residence, and administrator email/password login shall not parse or modify the password as a phone number.
- [ ] **PORTAL-LOGIN-007**: When APSG or CWDI login or verified registration handoff compares a submitted E.164 phone with a stored phone, the selected organization's server-side verification shall compare the complete country calling code and national number after removing presentation separators, shall recognize the equivalent Nigerian national number with or without one domestic leading `0` for a submitted `+234` value, and shall never compare suffixes or infer a country for an unprefixed non-Nigerian number.
- [ ] **PORTAL-LOGIN-008**: When a member signs in from localhost preview, the page shall verify the registration number and canonical E.164 phone using only the selected organization's phone-verification operation, permit view-only portal access after verification, and shall not read a member table directly, issue a write-capable credential, or perform member writes or uploads.
- [ ] **PORTAL-RECOVERY-001**: When a member searches for a forgotten registration number with a canonical E.164 phone, the recovery flow shall call `apsg_find_members_by_phone` and `cwdi_find_members_by_phone` independently, preserve the source registry on each result, and apply the selected organization's complete-number and Nigerian legacy matching rules without querying one registry through the other organization's operation.
- [ ] **PORTAL-RECOVERY-002**: Before a member confirms a recovery candidate, the recovery flow shall identify whether that candidate belongs to APSG or CWDI.
- [ ] **PORTAL-RECOVERY-003**: After a member confirms a recovery candidate and verifies its phone using the canonical E.164 value and selected organization's full-number rules, the recovery flow shall display only that candidate's organization-specific registration number and continue through the same organization-aware sign-in path.
- [ ] **PORTAL-RECOVERY-004**: When a member enters a phone number for registration-number recovery, the page shall validate it to one canonical E.164 value using the local phone metadata and accessible calling-code selector required by `PORTAL-LOGIN-006`; it shall reject an invalid number before either registry is queried and shall not send browser-generated phone candidates.
- [ ] **PORTAL-RECOVERY-005**: When a member supplies recovery location details, the page shall treat them only as optional candidate-ranking hints; a residence-country selector shall start blank and remain independent from the phone calling code, Nigeria shall use State/LGA/Ward, and a non-Nigeria residence shall use country/city/state-province separately from optional Nigerian home geography; a missing or changed location shall not suppress a full phone match or establish a member's identity.
- [ ] **PORTAL-RECOVERY-006**: If the phone, calling code, or a location hint changes while a recovery query or candidate confirmation is pending, the page shall discard stale results and candidate-verification state and use only the current request values.
- [ ] **PORTAL-RECOVERY-007**: When either registry returns multiple recovery candidates for the canonical E.164 phone, the page shall keep up to three candidates per available organization, rank location-hint matches before newer records, display each candidate's organization, and withhold the registration number until that candidate's phone is verified.
- [ ] **PORTAL-RECOVERY-008**: If one registry's recovery query fails while the other succeeds, the recovery page shall preserve the available registry's candidates, identify the unavailable organization and allow retry, and shall not claim that both organizations have no match; it shall report recovery as unavailable only when neither registry can be queried.

## Member profile and photo editing

- [ ] **PORTAL-EDIT-001**: In verified CWDI member mode outside localhost preview, the member shall be able to edit only the allowlisted basic profile fields and replace a profile photo through CWDI-specific secure write endpoints; CWDI bank details shall remain read-only.
- [ ] **PORTAL-EDIT-002**: In verified APSG member mode outside localhost preview, the member shall be able to edit only the allowlisted basic profile fields and replace a profile photo through APSG-specific secure write endpoints; existing APSG bank-detail editing shall remain available through its existing RPC; authorized administrator editing shall retain its existing behavior.
- [ ] **PORTAL-EDIT-003**: When a verified APSG or CWDI member opens member details from localhost, the page shall render the verified session snapshot in view-only mode without waiting for a duplicate registry lookup or administrator session, issue no write-capable member credential, disable member profile, account-detail and photo edits, and issue no member-row update, RPC write or storage upload from member controls; an absent verified session shall display the member login gate.
- [ ] **PORTAL-EDIT-004**: When a verified APSG or CWDI member edits their profile, the form shall allow first name, middle name, last name, gender (`sex`) and email; current residence, Nigerian home location, designation, phone, registration date, registration number, PVC/APC identifiers, uploaded identity documents and membership classification shall remain read-only in member mode.
- [ ] **PORTAL-EDIT-005**: When a verified APSG or CWDI member saves profile edits, the page shall validate the form, submit only changed allowlisted fields, prevent duplicate saves, report success or failure beside the form, and show success only after the organization-specific endpoint confirms the update; Cancel shall discard the draft, and leaving with unsaved changes shall require confirmation before discarding.
- [ ] **PORTAL-EDIT-006**: If another edit changes the member record after the profile form loads, then the profile endpoint shall reject the stale revision and the page shall preserve the member's draft, show the latest saved values, and require explicit reconciliation before another save.
- [ ] **PORTAL-EDIT-007**: When a verified APSG or CWDI member selects a replacement profile photo, the page shall show a local preview with Save and Cancel controls and accept JPEG, PNG or WebP images no larger than 3 MB; the server shall also validate the image content and size.
- [ ] **PORTAL-EDIT-008**: When a verified APSG or CWDI member saves a replacement profile photo, the photo endpoint shall use an idempotency key, generate an object key inside that member's organization-specific storage path, check the record revision, upload the new photo before changing the database pointer, retain the old photo until the pointer update commits, and best-effort clean up unreferenced or superseded objects only inside that member's storage path; a cleanup failure after commit shall not undo the saved photo; once upload begins, the page shall prevent duplicate submissions and shall not imply that Cancel can roll the upload back.
- [ ] **PORTAL-EDIT-009**: If a profile or photo write fails, conflicts, times out or returns an unknown result, then the page shall preserve the member's draft and reconcile against the authoritative record before retrying; it shall never fall back to direct browser table updates or anonymous storage uploads.
- [ ] **PORTAL-EDIT-010**: While a verified APSG or CWDI member edits their profile, the phone number shall remain read-only; a separate phone-change flow shall not save a proposed number until control of that number has been verified through an approved delivery channel, and shall state that the change is unavailable while no delivery channel is configured.
- [ ] **PORTAL-EDIT-011**: When a verified APSG member saves bank details, the page shall use the existing `update_member_account_details` RPC; if the RPC is unavailable, the page shall retain the saved values and show an error instead of falling back to a direct table update; CWDI bank details shall remain read-only.
- [ ] **PORTAL-EDIT-012**: When a verified APSG or CWDI member profile or photo write response omits read-only contact fields, the page shall preserve them from the verified profile snapshot or refresh the record from the selected registry and shall not interpret omitted fields as `null`.
- [ ] **PORTAL-EDIT-013**: When a verified APSG or CWDI member edits their profile, `nigerian_phone_e164` and `preferred_contact_type` shall remain read-only alongside the primary phone.

## Member portal authorization and data access

- [ ] **PORTAL-SECURITY-001**: The CWDI registry shall have row-level security enabled, grant direct table privileges only to `authenticated`, and permit direct access only when `public.is_admin_at_least('admin')` succeeds; public CWDI registration and member lookup shall use the CWDI-specific SECURITY DEFINER RPCs.
- [ ] **PORTAL-SECURITY-002**: When a member calls a profile or photo write endpoint, the endpoint shall verify the signed member credential and derive the member ID, organization registry and permitted write scope from verified claims; caller-supplied member IDs, table names, storage paths, URL parameters and browser session flags shall not grant write authority.
- [ ] **PORTAL-SECURITY-003**: Member session and write endpoints shall keep signing and service-role secrets server-side, rate-limit verification and write requests, and avoid logging credentials, registration numbers or phone numbers.
- [ ] **PORTAL-SECURITY-004**: When a profile or photo endpoint returns an error, it shall distinguish expired credentials, forbidden fields, validation errors, revision conflicts, rate limits and service failures without exposing credentials or protected member data.
- [ ] **PORTAL-SECURITY-005**: When a verified APSG or CWDI member record is returned in production, `nigerian_phone_e164` and `preferred_contact_type` shall appear only in the server-owned verified member-view response allowlist used by session and refresh paths; in localhost preview the portal shall render them from the verified session snapshot, and in both modes shall exclude them from signed credentials, URLs, logs, and profile or photo write responses.

## Member-facing presentation

- [ ] **PORTAL-VIEW-001**: When a verified CWDI member opens member details, the member-facing confirmation shall identify CWDI and use the CWDI logo.
- [ ] **PORTAL-VIEW-002**: When an APSG or CWDI member record is displayed in member details, the page and copied summary shall include Middle Name and Gender from `middle_name` and `sex`, show an em dash for missing values, and compose a full name using first, middle and last name.
- [ ] **PORTAL-VIEW-003**: While verified APSG or CWDI member mode is active, the page shall show the selected organization's name and logo with the member identity, profile details, documents and account details, and shall hide Record Summary (including PVC/APC status, record-health and dossier-readiness metrics, and admin contact actions) and Checklist & Audit Trail; administrator mode shall retain those panels and actions.
- [ ] **PORTAL-VIEW-004**: While verified APSG or CWDI member mode is active, the page shall hide the public-site footer and show one labeled Home action linking to `/` at every viewport width; activating Home shall navigate without logging out the member or changing the displayed record.
- [ ] **PORTAL-VIEW-005**: At viewport widths from 320px through 767px, verified APSG or CWDI member mode shall use a single-column profile layout, keep the Home label visible, wrap header actions when needed, and prevent horizontal page scrolling; at every viewport width, portal controls shall remain fully visible and at least 44px high, and long names, registration numbers, addresses and filenames shall wrap without clipping.
- [ ] **PORTAL-VIEW-006**: When a verified APSG or CWDI member profile has no photo or uploaded document, the page shall show a compact placeholder; if profile data fails to load, the page shall keep Home navigation available and show its existing error feedback.
- [ ] **PORTAL-VIEW-007**: In verified APSG or CWDI member mode, keyboard users shall be able to operate Home, Print, Copy and permitted edit actions with visible focus; organization logos shall have meaningful alternative text, and status indicators shall include text labels that remain understandable without color.
- [ ] **PORTAL-VIEW-008**: When a member profile is displayed, the page shall show verified current residence separately from Nigerian home location: for `NGA`, show Nigeria with the current Nigerian State/LGA/Ward; for another residence country, show its local-metadata country name, city and state/province and label any State/LGA/Ward as Nigerian home location; when the residence country is absent, show it as not recorded and shall not infer current residence from legacy location, phone or registration number.
- [ ] **PORTAL-VIEW-009**: When a verified APSG or CWDI member profile and copied summary show contact information, regardless of residence, they shall show the primary phone and, only when the stored Nigerian number is valid E.164 and its preference is `primary` or `nigerian`, show the Nigerian number with its readable preferred-contact label; when the number is absent, omit the secondary number and preference, and when the stored pair is inconsistent, hide both while keeping the primary visible and showing existing profile data-error feedback.
- [ ] **PORTAL-VIEW-010**: When Copy is activated in verified member mode, including during an unsaved profile edit, the page shall copy only saved verified profile values and shall announce success or clipboard failure through accessible feedback.
