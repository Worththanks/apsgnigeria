# Membership Registration Requirements

- [ ] **REG-UI-001**: On the APSG and CWDI registration page, the progress indicator shall display Membership, Your details, and Review & submit in three ordered columns, with each numbered marker above its label.
- [ ] **REG-UI-002**: On the APSG and CWDI registration page, each progress connector shall stop before the next numbered marker and shall not cross any stage label.
- [ ] **REG-UI-003**: When the APSG/CWDI registration stage changes, its progress indicator shall visually distinguish the active, completed and upcoming stages and expose only the active stage with `aria-current="step"`.
- [ ] **REG-UI-004**: At narrow viewport widths on APSG/CWDI registration, the progress indicator shall allow labels to wrap within their columns, keep connectors aligned with the numbered markers, and avoid horizontal page overflow.
- [ ] **REG-UI-005**: When reduced motion is enabled on APSG/CWDI registration, its progress indicator shall preserve stage styling without animated marker transitions.

## Welcome and Membership Choice

- [x] **REG-UI-036**: When APSG/CWDI registration opens at the Membership stage, the first screen shall explain that the applicant chooses one organization and membership type per registration, that the other organization requires a separate registration, and that residence and contact details follow the choice.
- [ ] **REG-UI-037**: On the APSG/CWDI Membership stage, the four existing choices shall form one named radiogroup with one shared radio name and retain visual APSG and CWDI parent-organization groups; organization names shall remain visible independently of decorative logos; below 40rem each organization's general and Youth Wing cards shall stack vertically, at 40rem and above their options shall appear side by side, and parent groups shall reflow without horizontal overflow or reordered choices at 320px and enlarged text sizes.
- [x] **REG-UI-038**: When the APSG/CWDI Membership stage is visible, the page shall retain the member sign-in link and place a reachable informational note between the organization groups and Continue that explains an existing member who chooses that organization's Youth Wing option continues through the existing verification path, without adding another action, membership selector, or registration state.
- [x] **REG-UI-039**: On first entry to APSG/CWDI registration and after its completed-registration reset, no membership shall be preselected and Continue shall remain disabled until a radio is selected; when a choice is activated or changed through native radio-key interaction, selection styling and Continue availability shall update synchronously without navigation or lookup, while returning from details to change membership shall retain the prior choice until replaced.

## Details and Review Grouping

- [ ] **REG-UI-040**: When APSG/CWDI registration details are displayed, presentation shall group the existing fields under Personal details, Residence, and Contact in that order; Contact shall place the required primary phone and email before any field-component-supplied diaspora Nigerian phone and preferred-contact control, without inferring residence from either phone's calling code.
- [ ] **REG-UI-041**: When APSG/CWDI registration details are displayed, fields shall stack in one column below 48rem; at 48rem and above, two-column pairs shall be First/Middle Name, Last Name/Gender, Level/Designation, primary phone/email, and current city/state-province, while residence country spans the row; Nigerian location controls shall use two columns from 48rem to 64rem with Ward spanning the row and three columns from 64rem, and enlarged text or narrow effective viewports shall reflow without horizontal scrolling or a blank cell when an optional control is absent.
- [ ] **REG-UI-042**: When APSG/CWDI registration review is displayed, its Personal details, Residence, and Contact groups shall match the details order; current residence shall remain distinct from Nigerian home location, and primary phone, any supplied Nigerian contact number, and preferred-contact choice shall be shown separately while an unsupplied optional number is omitted.
- [ ] **REG-UI-043**: If a residence-driven field-visibility update hides the focused control on APSG/CWDI registration, presentation shall move focus to the current-residence selector after the update, keep the details stage active, and retain the Residence viewport position without clearing remaining values; the field component shall own hidden-value retention.

## Frame and Actions

- [ ] **REG-UI-006**: On APSG/CWDI registration, the focused frame shall expose Home, Help/Contact and Member login in a compact masthead without a public navbar, browsing dock or footer.
- [ ] **REG-UI-007**: On APSG/CWDI registration, the frame shall use maximum width 960px, 16px phone gutters and 24px larger-screen gutters, a single-column phone form and only related short two-column fields on wider screens, while preserving field IDs/names, requiredness, organization branding and payload owners.
- [ ] **REG-UI-008**: On APSG/CWDI registration, the four existing mutually exclusive membership choices shall retain their parent-organization grouping, visible identity, whole-label activation and readable layout at 320px, with Continue disabled until exactly one choice is selected.
- [ ] **REG-UI-009**: On APSG/CWDI registration, the original action controls shall present Continue at membership choice, Review details as the primary details action, Submit directly as a secondary in-flow action, Edit details and Confirm & submit in review, and Done on the success receipt without cloned action handlers.
- [ ] **REG-UI-010**: Below 48rem on APSG/CWDI registration, the current primary action shall occupy a bottom action area only while no dialog/likely keyboard is active, visual viewport height is at least 480px, and the rendered area is at most one quarter of that height; otherwise it shall remain in flow.
- [ ] **REG-UI-011**: When the APSG/CWDI registration action area's size or placement changes, presentation shall measure its height, reserve matching form-end/scroll space plus safe-area clearance for bottom mode, and recalculate for resized viewports, orientation and enlarged content; at 48rem and above actions shall remain in flow.
- [ ] **REG-UI-012**: When APSG/CWDI registration detects keyboard mode, it shall require editable focus, visualViewport scale from 0.9 through 1.1 and a height reduction greater than 140px from the current orientation's unobstructed reference; it shall record that reference only without editable focus at that scale, rebuild it after orientation changes, refresh it after blur/stabilization and ignore pinch zoom alone.
- [ ] **REG-UI-013**: If visualViewport is unavailable on APSG/CWDI registration, editable focus shall place actions in flow, and focus/viewport recovery shall recalculate eligible action placement.
- [ ] **REG-UI-014**: While APSG/CWDI registration prepares review through its lookup owner, the busy controller shall block competing review/direct-submit actions and membership changes, expose aria-busy on the active action and allow personal-field corrections without launching another presentation-owned request.
- [ ] **REG-UI-015**: When APSG/CWDI registration identity or membership changes during review preparation, the presentation shall invalidate that pending presentation through the lookup owner so stale completion cannot change stage, open a dialog or replace newer feedback; a current successful lookup shall capture the latest remaining form values for review.
- [ ] **REG-UI-016**: When APSG/CWDI registration receives a form submit from Enter, it shall follow the Review details validation/lookup path without page navigation or a direct registry write, while retaining normal textarea and select keyboard behavior.

## Validation, Dialogs and Focus

- [ ] **REG-UI-017**: If an APSG/CWDI registration action fails existing field validation, presentation shall retain values, mark affected controls with aria-invalid and associated inline messages, and focus an error summary with links to the affected controls.
- [ ] **REG-UI-018**: On APSG/CWDI registration, formMessage and registrationLookupStatus shall retain distinct outcome/lookup roles, preserve accessible async lookup announcements, wrap within the available width and expose no additional candidate identity or false no-match outcome.
- [ ] **REG-UI-019**: On APSG/CWDI registration, one presenter shall use a single physical native dialog frame where supported with one active review, duplicate-confirmation or success section retaining the existing content IDs/actions, a scrollable body, reachable actions, visible-viewport sizing, 12px clearance and safe-area padding.
- [ ] **REG-UI-020**: When APSG/CWDI registration switches review, duplicate-confirmation or success content, presentation shall keep the same frame modal, activate only the new section, update its accessible label, focus its heading and hide the page action area while the frame is active; explicit Edit/dismiss/Done/error rules shall determine when the frame closes.
- [ ] **REG-UI-021**: When Continue advances a valid APSG/CWDI membership choice, presentation shall show details, set stage two, focus the first detail field and place its heading below the masthead through the existing stage handler.
- [ ] **REG-UI-022**: When APSG/CWDI registration successfully prepares a current review snapshot, presentation shall set stage three, open review and focus its heading without bypassing the current lookup gate.
- [ ] **REG-UI-023**: When Edit details closes APSG/CWDI registration review, presentation shall return to stage two and focus the first editable field; when Escape/backdrop closes review before submission, it shall return to stage two and restore Review details.
- [ ] **REG-UI-024**: When APSG/CWDI lookup reports a candidate conflict, presentation shall show the existing duplicate flow without opening review behind it; dismissing that dialog shall restore phone-field focus and retain conflict status and entered values under the lookup contract.
- [ ] **REG-UI-025**: While an APSG/CWDI registration submit operation is in flight, presentation shall reflect the submission owner's snapshot/control lock, prevent dialog dismissal and competing actions, and retain its values and snapshot until completion or failure.
- [ ] **REG-UI-026**: If APSG/CWDI direct submission fails or local preview blocks it, presentation shall keep details and feedback at stage two; if submission from review fails or is blocked, it shall retain stage three and announce/focus the error inside review with Edit/Retry available.
- [ ] **REG-UI-027**: When an APSG/CWDI review error links to an underlying form field, activating the correction link shall close review, restore stage two and then focus/scroll to that field after details are available rather than focusing through an inert background.
- [ ] **REG-UI-028**: When APSG/CWDI submission returns success, presentation shall show the existing organization-specific receipt, focus its heading and keep it visible on Escape/backdrop until Done explicitly continues.
- [ ] **REG-UI-029**: When Done is activated on APSG/CWDI registration success, presentation shall run the existing submission-owned reset, return to stage one and focus the first membership choice.
- [ ] **REG-UI-030**: If native dialog support is unavailable on APSG/CWDI registration, the same single-frame/content-switching model shall provide a labeled modal fallback, focus trap, inert background where supported and the same state transitions, with the closed frame hidden/inert and focus restored to a connected visible owner or current stage heading.

- [ ] **REG-UI-034**: When Confirm is activated on APSG/CWDI registration, presentation shall compare the reviewed membership, field values and selected files with current form state before invoking submission; a mismatch shall block that attempt, close to details, retain values, explain that fresh review is required and require the existing validation/lookup gate again, while direct submission retains its current-value path and operation lock.

## Preview and Motion

- [ ] **REG-UI-031**: When APSG/CWDI registration is served as a local preview, presentation shall preserve the existing guards against uploads, number allocation, inserts, updates and portal handoff as if registration submission succeeded; after separate verification of an existing member, it shall allow that organization-specific portal login in view-only mode, retain the form on a blocked registration action, and report preview status without simulating membership success or an issued ID.
- [ ] **REG-UI-032**: On APSG/CWDI registration, presentation changes shall consume organization, matching, field, upload and receipt data through their existing owners and shall not add client-side persistence of identity fields.
- [ ] **REG-UI-033**: When reduced motion is enabled on APSG/CWDI registration, page/dialog/action transitions and focus-related scrolling shall preserve visible state without animation or smooth scrolling.
- [ ] **REG-UI-035**: When APSG/CWDI registration renders applicant-provided names, contact details, designation, verification numbers or filenames in review, the page shall display them as text and shall not interpret their characters as HTML.
