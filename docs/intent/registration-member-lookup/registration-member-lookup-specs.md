# Registration Existing-Member Lookup Requirements

- [ ] **REG-MATCH-001**: When an applicant has selected exactly one of APSG, APSG Youth Wing, CWDI, or CWDI Youth Wing and entered a valid E.164 phone number plus first and last name, the registration page shall perform a read-only lookup only in that selected organization's registry, using its organization-specific phone-lookup RPC and sending the single canonical E.164 value.
- [ ] **REG-MATCH-002**: When the selected organization's registry contains a full-number phone match with the applicant's normalized first and last name, the registration page shall identify that organization as an existing membership and explain that the existing record will be reused; when Youth Wing is newly selected for that record, the page shall explain that the existing record will be classified as Youth Wing during submission.
- [ ] **REG-MATCH-004**: If the selected APSG or CWDI registry contains a full-number phone match but none of its records has the applicant's normalized first and last name, then as soon as the lookup completes—including a lookup triggered by phone-field blur—the registration page shall show a non-identifying conflict status and a candidate-confirmation dialog with up to three location-ranked candidates from that registry; a confirmed candidate shall require registration-number-and-phone verification against the same registry using the canonical E.164 value and the registry's full-number comparison rules, while rejecting all candidates shall show that organization's coordinator-contact step and keep the current registration blocked; successful verification for that organization's Youth Wing shall pass the verified candidate to submission for a Youth Wing-only update; successful verification for general membership shall open that candidate's organization-specific member portal without asking the applicant to enter the same details again. In production, the portal handoff shall re-verify the candidate through its session endpoint and obtain a scoped member credential before opening the portal; if credential issuance fails, the candidate shall remain verified and the registration page shall show a retryable portal-entry error. From local preview, the verified handoff shall open the view-only portal without a write-capable credential.
- [ ] **REG-MATCH-005**: If the selected organization's registry lookup fails, the registration page shall display a retryable, organization-specific lookup error, shall not describe the applicant as unregistered, and shall block review and submission for the current registration until lookup succeeds. If the CWDI lookup returns `PGRST205`, the page shall identify `cwdi_members` as unavailable and shall not query APSG's `members` table as a fallback.
- [ ] **REG-MATCH-006**: When the phone number, first name, last name, or selected membership changes while a registration lookup is pending or after one completes, the registration page shall disregard stale results and use the current values for review and submission checks.
- [ ] **REG-MATCH-007**: When the APSG and CWDI registration page is served from localhost, the page shall perform the same read-only full-number lookup using one canonical E.164 value; after successful registration-number-and-phone verification under the same full-number rules for general membership, it shall permit the selected organization's member-portal handoff in view-only mode without issuing a write-capable credential, while continuing to block registration inserts, Youth Wing updates, and file uploads.
- [ ] **REG-MATCH-008**: When the registration lookup status changes on the APSG and CWDI registration page, the page shall announce the status through an accessible live region and shall not disclose a matched member's registration number or other record details in that status.
- [ ] **REG-MATCH-009**: If the APSG phone-lookup RPC is unavailable, the registration page shall display a retryable APSG lookup error, shall not query the `members` table directly, and shall not report that the applicant has no matching record.
- [ ] **REG-MATCH-011**: If the CWDI phone-lookup RPC `cwdi_find_members_by_phone` is unavailable, the registration page shall report a CWDI lookup failure, shall not query `cwdi_members` directly from the browser, and shall not treat the failure as a no-match result.
- [ ] **REG-MATCH-012**: If the CWDI registration-number-and-phone verification RPC `cwdi_member_login_by_reg_phone` is unavailable, the registration page shall fail verification without querying `cwdi_members` directly from the browser; when the RPC succeeds, CWDI Youth Wing shall pass the verified candidate to submission for a Youth Wing-only update and general CWDI membership shall preserve the CWDI-specific member-portal handoff without a second login prompt, including the view-only localhost handoff.
- [ ] **REG-MATCH-013**: If the registration page has no selected membership or more than one selected membership, then the page shall block lookup, review, and submission until exactly one of APSG, APSG Youth Wing, CWDI, or CWDI Youth Wing is selected.
- [ ] **REG-MATCH-014**: When the selected membership changes while the candidate-confirmation dialog is open, the page shall close the dialog, discard the candidate and verification state, and perform any subsequent lookup only against the newly selected organization's registry.
- [ ] **REG-MATCH-015**: When the applicant has entered a valid phone number but has not entered both first and last name, the registration page shall prompt for the missing name fields and shall not query either organization registry.
- [ ] **REG-MATCH-017**: When multiple records in the selected registry match the applicant's canonical E.164 phone under the full-number rules and have the same normalized first and last name, the registration page shall identify the newest matching record as the existing member.
- [ ] **REG-MATCH-018**: If the APSG registration-number-and-phone verification RPC is unavailable, the registration page shall fail verification and shall not query the `members` table directly; when APSG verification succeeds, APSG Youth Wing shall pass the verified candidate to submission for a Youth Wing-only update, while general APSG membership shall preserve the APSG member-portal handoff without a second login prompt, including the view-only localhost handoff.
- [ ] **REG-MATCH-019**: When a valid E.164 phone value leaves the phone field and first and last name are available, or before review or direct submission when the current identity and membership selection have not already been checked successfully, the registration page shall run the selected-registry lookup; it may reuse an in-flight or completed result only while the canonical E.164 phone, first name, last name, and selected membership remain unchanged.
- [ ] **REG-MATCH-020**: When either organization's lookup or registration-number-and-phone verification RPC receives a phone value, it shall accept one syntactically valid E.164 value and compare the complete country calling code and national number after removing presentation separators from stored values; it shall not compare suffixes, truncate digits, or accept a browser-generated list of alternate phone candidates.
- [ ] **REG-MATCH-021**: When the submitted canonical E.164 phone is Nigerian (`+234`), the selected organization's lookup and candidate-verification RPCs shall also match a legacy Nigerian national phone whose complete national significant number is identical, with or without one domestic leading `0`.
- [ ] **REG-MATCH-022**: When the submitted canonical E.164 phone is not Nigerian (`+234`), the selected organization's lookup and candidate-verification RPCs shall match only a stored phone with an explicit international country calling code whose complete normalized E.164 digits equal the submitted value; they shall not infer a country for an unprefixed national phone or match by the final digits.
- [ ] **REG-MATCH-023**: If an organization's lookup or candidate-verification RPC receives a malformed phone value or more than one phone candidate, it shall reject the request before querying member records.
