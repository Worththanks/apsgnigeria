About CWDI

Building confident women for leadership, service and community progress.

Confident Women Development Initiative (CWDI) exists to strengthen women’s participation in development, public service, civic engagement, family wellbeing, education, entrepreneurship and grassroots mobilisation.

The initiative supports women with the confidence, structure, mentorship and public platform required to contribute meaningfully to national progress while promoting values of dignity, inclusion, responsibility and service.

Vision

To raise confident women who can lead, serve, organise and contribute to sustainable community and national development.

Mission

To empower women through advocacy, mentorship, civic education, leadership opportunities and community-focused programmes.

Mandate

Women development, social support, leadership representation, grassroots engagement and inclusive progress.

Community

Connecting matrons, patrons, executives, volunteers and community women under one development structure.

What CWDI Stands For

These sections explain the kind of work CWDI can publicly communicate before visitors proceed to the official directory.

Leadership Training

Mentorship, public confidence, women leadership orientation and responsibility development.

Economic Empowerment

Entrepreneurship support, practical skills, cooperative networks and women-led enterprise growth.

Family & Social Welfare

Community care, wellness awareness, social support and family-focused development activities.

Civic Participation

Encouraging women’s participation in public life, voter education, mobilisation and civic responsibility.
