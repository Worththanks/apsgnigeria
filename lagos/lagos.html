About APSG Lagos State

The Lagos State Chapter operates as the official state-level mobilisation and coordination arm of APSG Nigeria.

We are committed to:

  • Building disciplined grassroots structures across LGAs, LCDAs, and Wards
  • Enhancing inclusive political participation
  • Supporting institutional alignment within the APC ecosystem
  • Promoting measurable community impact through flagship projects

Core Pillars: Participation • Opportunity • Progress (Itesiwaju)
