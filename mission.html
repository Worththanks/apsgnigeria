Mission Statement

Mobilise, enlighten and unify Nigerians.

The mission of the APC Progressive Support Group (APSG) is to mobilize, enlighten, and unify Nigerians at home and abroad in support of the All Progressives Congress, fostering grassroots participation, promoting inclusive democracy, defending progressive values, and advancing policies and programmes that ensure peace, equity, prosperity, and sustainable national development.

Vision Statement

The foremost progressive support movement.

To be the foremost progressive support movement in Nigeria and the diaspora, uniting citizens under the ideals of the All Progressives Congress, and driving a future of national unity, inclusive democracy, good governance, and sustainable development for all Nigerians.

Core Values

The principles that guide APSG

Unity

Fostering inclusiveness and collective action across Nigeria's diversity.

Service

Committing to selfless service to the people and the nation.

Inclusiveness

Ensuring active participation of women, youth, workers, farmers, and persons with disabilities.

Progress

Promoting innovation, reform, and sustainable national development.

Integrity

Upholding transparency, honesty, and fairness in all engagements.

Accountability

Remaining answerable to members, the APC, and the people of Nigeria.

Patriotism

Promoting loyalty to Nigeria's unity, stability, and prosperity.

Collaboration

Building alliances with like-minded organizations for greater impact.

Education

Championing civic education and combating misinformation.
