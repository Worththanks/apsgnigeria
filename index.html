Who We Are

A disciplined movement for participation, opportunity and progress.

The APC Progressive Support Group (APSG) is a grassroots-driven mobilisation platform focused on strengthening progressive ideals, inclusive participation, and leadership committed to sustainable development beyond 2027.

This is not just a support group - it is a MOVEMENT built on Participation, Opportunity, and Progress (Itesiwaju).

We empower Nigerians - youth, women, workers, and communities across all states - to actively shape the future of the All Progressives Congress (APC) and our nation.

Confident Women Development Initiative

As the movement expanded, Ìyáàfin Oluwafunmilayo Adejoke Ogunba-Koleowo established the Confident Women Development Initiative (CWDI) as the official Women Wing of APSG — a strategic platform focused on empowering women socially, economically, and politically.

CWDI strengthens the APSG structure by developing women through confidence-building, skills, enterprise, financial literacy, mentorship, civic education, and inclusive participation.
